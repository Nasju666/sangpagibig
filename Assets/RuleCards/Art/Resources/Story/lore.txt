# Fragments are placed in front of the scene's shelves, in this order.

=== hub_charter | ArchiveHub | The Charter of Rules
Before the shelves, before the lamps, there were three sentences.

A thing pushed will move what it is bound to. Down is where the world agrees to fall. Air is nothing, until it is asked to be something.

The Archivists wrote them small, so they would fit in a hand, and called them Rule Cards.

=== hub_readers | ArchiveHub | On Readers
A rule unread is only ink. The Archivists wrote; the Readers read, and in the reading the rules became true.

When the last Archivist set down her pen, the Archive kept one Reader's chair warm, just in case. It has been warm a long time.

=== hub_index | ArchiveHub | Catalogue Note: Index Construct G
One (1) index construct. Small, runic, chronically curious. Knows the location of every volume. Does not know why it was built with an eye.

Has been reprimanded for reading during cataloguing. Has been reprimanded for humming. Do not let it near the Repository binding. It gets attached.

=== kin_rule | Stage_01_KineticLink | First Rule: Kinetic Link
Nothing in the Archive moves alone. Pull one book and its neighbours lean after it; lift a weight and somewhere a door remembers to rise.

The Kinetic Link is the rule of consequence. Without it, pushing is only a wish.

=== kin_log | Stage_01_KineticLink | Maintenance Log, Kinetic Wing
Day 4 since the Unbinding. Counterweights stopped answering their levers.

Day 6. The lattice diagrams on the walls are scrambling themselves. Colours cross where they never crossed.

Day 9. We have stopped going in. Whoever reads this: the diagram still knows the answer. It only wants every line to have somewhere to go.

=== kin_margin | Stage_01_KineticLink | Marginalia
Scrawled in a tiny, careful hand:

"If you hold the weight and walk, you are the link. Hello. This is Glyph. I am not supposed to write in the margins."

=== grav_rule | Stage_02_InvertGravity | Second Rule: Invert Gravity
Down is a promise the world makes to itself. In this wing the promise was written loosely, so it could be rewritten by anyone holding the card.

The Archivists used it to shelve the ceilings. There are more books on the ceilings than you would think.

=== grav_vault | Stage_02_InvertGravity | The Vault Glyphs
The gravity vault answers to three marks, read in order, the way a sentence is.

The Archivists hid the order nearby, so a Reader could find it and a thief could not. Thieves, it was assumed, do not read.

=== grav_complaint | Stage_02_InvertGravity | A Complaint
To the Office of Shelving.

An entire row of atlases fell UP this morning. I request either a net or a different career.

Archivist Orrin, Gravity Wing

=== air_rule | Stage_03_SolidifyAir | Third Rule: Solidify Air
Air is the patient rule. It holds nothing until a Reader insists, and then it holds just enough.

Three fields at a time, never more. The air tires, and tired air drops what it carries.

=== air_fields | Stage_03_SolidifyAir | On Reinforced Fields
Light fields carry a person. A core weighs more than a person, the way a secret weighs more than a sentence.

Reinforced fields are slower to raise and worth the wait. Many Archivists learned this from below.

=== air_unbinding | Stage_03_SolidifyAir | The Unbinding
No one tore the cards out. That is the part the Archivists never wrote down.

The rules were read so many times by no one, for so long, that they wore thin and slipped from the binding, like pages from a book left open in the rain.

=== rep_repository | Repository_Final | The Repository
Every rule the Archive has ever held passed through this room to be bound. The nodes do not judge what is written; they only check that it fits.

A complete book, the Archivists said, is a closed book. They meant it as a compliment.

=== rep_archivist | Repository_Final | The Last Archivist
She bound the final page herself and stepped out before the cover closed. Her note reads:

"Leave the door to a Reader. Leave the remembering to the index. One of them must go and one must stay, or the Archive will forget it was ever read."

=== rep_index | Repository_Final | Index Note, Unrequested
I have catalogued everything in the Archive except one thing, which is what happens to me when it closes.

I am choosing not to look it up. Some books are better read once.

G.
